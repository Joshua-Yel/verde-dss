# VERDE Manager Quick-Start Guide

## Purpose

This guide is for managers and team leads who need to get value from VERDE quickly. It focuses on the fastest way to understand business performance, spot issues, and act on the right information.

This is not a full technical manual. It is a practical operating guide for daily management.

## ARIA: the built-in decision assistant

VERDE includes ARIA, the AI assistant designed to help managers ask quick operational questions in plain language. Instead of manually reviewing multiple screens, you can ask direct questions like:

- Which services are strongest this month?
- What inventory is at risk?
- Which days need more staffing?
- Are we tracking to forecast?
- What is the biggest risk to profitability?

Use ARIA to speed up analysis, but always confirm the answer against the dashboard before making a final decision.

---

## 1. What the dashboard is built to do

VERDE helps managers answer five core questions:

- Are we making money?
- Are sales growing or falling?
- Which services are most important?
- Are we low on stock or exposing service risk?
- Do we have the right staffing to support demand?

It is designed to be a management cockpit for a salon or service business.

## 2. Your first 10 minutes in the system

### Step 1: Open Overview

Start with Overview. This is the most important page for a fast status check.

Look at:

- total revenue
- average daily revenue
- net income
- current forecast
- service mix
- low stock alerts

This gives you a quick read on the health of the business.

### Step 2: Check revenue trend

Ask:

- Is revenue rising or falling?
- Is the trend stronger than recent periods?
- Are we below the expected forecast?

If revenue is falling, review service demand and staffing quickly.

### Step 3: Review service demand

Open Service Demand.

Look for:

- services with rising demand
- services with falling demand
- seasonal or sudden changes

This helps decide whether to:

- schedule more staff
- increase promotion
- adjust pricing or service mix
- train staff on a growing service

### Step 4: Review inventory risk

Open Inventory.

Look for:

- low stock items
- items below reorder threshold
- projected shortages

This is a fast way to prevent service disruption.

### Step 5: Review staffing alignment

Open Staffing.

Look for:

- busy days or periods that need more consistent staffing
- underutilized periods that may require schedule changes
- service bottlenecks

### Step 6: Review financial health

Open Financials.

Look for:

- revenue vs expense growth
- net margin pressure
- forecasted profit change

### Step 7: Ask ARIA if needed

If you need a quick business summary, ask ARIA a direct question such as:

- “Which services are growing fastest?”
- “What inventory is at risk?”
- “Which days are busiest?”
- “Are we forecasted to improve next month?”

## 4. What to inspect every day

For a daily management routine, review these items:

### Revenue performance

- are sales running above or below recent levels?
- is there any obvious drop in activity?

### Service activity

- which services are busiest?
- which services are declining?

### Inventory risk

- which products are low?
- which items need a reorder now?

### Staffing balance

- are busy periods covered?
- are there empty or overstaffed periods?

### Forecast status

- are we tracking toward expected performance?
- are customers booking at a different pace than expected?

---

## 5. Daily quick workflow

### Morning review (10 minutes)

1. Open Overview.
2. Scan KPI cards and revenue trend.
3. Check if there are any low-stock alerts.
4. Check service demand for the strongest and weakest services.
5. Review staffing coverage by day or time block.
6. Note any issues to follow up on.

### Midday operational check

1. Look for sudden demand shifts.
2. Confirm whether inventory is still healthy.
3. Review outstanding risks with the team.

### End-of-day review

1. Review revenue vs forecast.
2. Compare service demand and staffing patterns.
3. Capture operational issues for the next day.

---

## 6. Manager decision-making points

### If revenue is falling

Check:

- service demand trends
- recent changes in appointment volume
- whether staffing coverage is weak
- whether inventory is affecting sales

### If demand is rising

Check:

- which service is growing
- whether inventory is sufficient
- whether staffing can support the demand
- whether training is needed

### If inventory is low

Check:

- urgency of reorder
- expected demand over the next period
- which supplier or item is involved
- whether the risk affects customer experience

### If staffing is mismatched

Check:

- busiest days or periods
- overstaffed slow periods
- service bottlenecks
- schedule reset needs

## 7. How to use reports

Use the Reports tool when you need a clean summary for meetings, review sessions, or team updates.

### Exporting a report

1. Click Reports in the sidebar.
2. Select the sections you want.
3. Choose PDF or Excel.
4. Export the file.

### Recommended usage

- PDF: executive summary, presentation, management review
- Excel: analyzing numbers or sharing with external partners

### Good practice

Do not export everything unless you need it. Export only the sections that support the decision or meeting.

## 9. What to watch weekly

Managers should review the following each week:

- revenue movement
- demand growth by service

- staffing imbalance
- forecast variance
- profit trend

These are the signals that help you act before problems become costly.

---

## 10. What the numbers mean

### Overview metrics

These are not just abstract numbers. They should be tied to action.

Examples:

- lower revenue than expected → check demand and staffing
- high service concentration → assess dependence on one service
- low inventory → reorder before disruption
- poor forecast trend → review assumptions or recent demand shifts

### Service demand metrics

Show whether your offerings are attracting demand or losing momentum.

### Inventory metrics

Show whether the business is prepared for upcoming demand.

### Financial metrics

Show whether operations are sustainable and profit-positive.

### Staffing metrics

Show whether labor placement aligns with real business need.

---

## 11. Common manager mistakes

Avoid these habits:

- checking only one metric without context
- ignoring inventory risk because the service is busy today
- using weekly data without comparing to trend
- ignoring low staffing during high-demand periods
- relying on AI without validating the numbers
