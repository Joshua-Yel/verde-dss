# VERDE Client-Facing Guide

## A smarter view of your business operations

VERDE is a business intelligence dashboard built for modern service businesses. It brings together your most important operational information in one place so you can understand what is happening, what is likely to happen next, and where action is needed.

This guide is written for business owners, leadership teams, and decision-makers who want a polished, easy-to-understand overview of how the system supports smarter operations.

## ARIA: the built-in business assistant

VERDE also includes ARIA, an AI assistant that can answer operational questions in plain language. This gives leadership a faster way to ask questions such as:

- Which service is growing fastest?
- Are we likely to run low on stock this month?
- What is the current financial forecast?
- Are we understaffed during busy periods?
- What should we focus on next?

ARIA supports decision-making by turning dashboard data into quick business insight without forcing users to manually search through reports.

## 1. What VERDE does

VERDE helps businesses manage the full operational picture across four major areas:

- sales and revenue performance
- service demand and customer activity
- inventory and product availability
- staffing and labor planning

It also includes a financial forecasting layer and an AI assistant that can help answer operational questions in plain language.

The result is a dashboard that supports both daily decisions and longer-term planning.

## 5. Overview: the executive summary

The Overview page is the home base of the system. It is designed to give leadership a quick and accurate view of business health.

### What it shows

- KPI cards summarizing business activity
- revenue trend over time
- service contribution mix
- daily operational activity

### Why it matters

This is the page most people use to understand whether the business is healthy, stable, or under stress.

### What leadership should look for

- Is revenue improving or declining?
- Are there strong or weak services?
- Are there low-stock issues?
- Are there signs of under- or over-staffing?

This page prevents the team from making decisions based on anecdotal information alone.

---

## 6. Service Demand: understanding demand and growth

The Service Demand page focuses on service activity patterns.

### Why it matters

This is the page for understanding what customers are actually buying and which services are growing in importance.

### What it helps answer

- Which services have the strongest demand?
- Which services are falling behind?
- What is the likely demand trend next month?
- Which parts of the business need added attention?

### Why this matters strategically

The business can increase revenue by focusing on the right service lines, improving scheduling, and allocating staff where demand is strongest.

---

## 7. Inventory: reducing operational risk

The Inventory page helps the business maintain healthy stock levels without overstocking.

### Why it matters

Stock-outs can interrupt service quality, reduce customer satisfaction, and reduce sales. Overstocking can create unnecessary cash burden.

### What the system helps with

- identifying low stock items
- highlighting reorder urgency
- tracking expected inventory levels
- forecasting next month’s stock position

### What leadership should look for

- Are key items running low?
- Are reorder thresholds being met?
- Are there signs of future stockout risk?

This function helps the business keep operations resilient and efficient.

---

## 8. Financials: tracking money and margin

The Financials area helps the business understand how revenue and expenses are interacting.

### What it shows

- revenue trend
- expense trend
- net income and profitability signal
- forecasted financial performance

### Why it matters

A business can look strong on sales but still be under pressure if costs are growing faster than revenue. Financials provides that important operational check.

### Decision value

This section supports:

- pricing review
- cost control decisions
- forecast-based planning
- margin protection

---

## 9. Staffing: balancing labor with demand

The Staffing page connects labor to actual business activity.

### Why it matters

Labor is one of the largest operating costs in a service business. If staffing is not aligned with demand, the business loses efficiency.

### What it helps uncover

- busy periods that may need more support
- quiet periods where staffing could be reduced
- schedule inefficiencies
- labor stress points during service-heavy days

### Why leadership cares

The right staffing plan improves service quality, protects productivity, and reduces wasted labor cost.

## 14. How to interpret the numbers correctly

The dashboard is most useful when interpreted as a trend, not as a single isolated value.

### A single high number is not always good

For example:

- strong revenue in one day may be a temporary spike
- a low inventory number may be normal in one context but risky in another
- a busy week without stronger profitability could still be inefficient

### Trend matters more than snapshot

The most valuable decisions often come from understanding:

- direction
- momentum
- risk
- comparison to recent periods

This is why the dashboard is designed around trends, forecasts, and summaries.
